FAQ

Questions,answered.

Do you work with clients outside Texas?

Yes. Our office is in Missouri City, TX, and we work with individuals and businesses in all 50 states. Documents are exchanged through our secure client portal, so you don’t need to visit in person.

Can I work with you in Vietnamese?

Yes. We serve clients in both English and Vietnamese.

How much will my tax return cost?

Individual returns start at $200 and business bookkeeping starts at $500/month (see Pricing above). Your exact fee depends on your situation and is confirmed in writing before any work begins.

I received a letter from the IRS. Can you help?

Yes. Tony Hoang is an IRS Enrolled Agent, licensed to represent taxpayers before the IRS in audits, collections and appeals. Get in touch and we’ll explain what the letter means and what your options are.

How do I send you my documents?

Through our secure client portal (the Login button at the top of the page). Please don’t send Social Security numbers or tax documents by regular email.

What’s the difference between a CPA and an EA?

A CPA is licensed by a state board of accountancy and covers accounting, audit and tax. An Enrolled Agent is licensed by the IRS and specializes in tax. Having both on one team means your books, your tax strategy and any IRS issues are handled in one place.
